Print settings

  • Resin printer recommended. The web between pogo holes is only 1.27 − 0.70 = 0.57 mm, which is too thin for a 0.4 mm FDM nozzle.
  • Orientation: tip face on the plate, no supports inside the holes. Holes print vertical and round.
  • Resin: tough or ABS-like, or high-temp resin if you solder close to the plates.
  • Holes: print at nominal size, then ream by hand with carbide PCB drills, Ø0.70 for pogo and Ø0.80 for guides. Resin usually prints small holes 0.03–0.08 undersize.
  • FDM fallback: 0.2 mm nozzle, ASA or PETG, holes modelled Ø0.5 and drilled out. Expect a weaker web.
  • Print one tolerance test first: the holder plus the depth jig take about 20 minutes on most MSLA printers.

Assembly sequence

  1. Ream all 9 holes. Pins should slide in with light finger force.
  2. Put the holder flange-down in the depth jig. Push 3 guide wires (cut 18 mm long) and 6 pogo pins in from the tip side until they bottom out. This sets them 1.5 mm behind the flange.
  3. Wick thin CA glue at the tip face and the flange. Keep glue off the plungers.
  4. Trim the guides to 6.0 mm in front of the tip face and bevel the ends. Pogo tips then sit at 4.85, about 1.15 behind the guides.
  5. Thread each wire through its flange notch. Solder it to the barrel inside the solder zone, at least 0.6 mm away from the front plate. Use a fine tip, pre-tinned wire and a short dwell time.
  6. Slide on the 4.5 mm heat-shrink, seat the flange in the slot of case half v2, glue the halves together, then add the 6 mm heat-shrink and the cover as in the original guide.

Open points for review

  • Heat Soldering heats the barrels inside the plastic. Resin handles short hits; PETG may soften. Option: crimp micro ferrules instead of soldering.
  • Pin length The 4.85 mm tip protrusion assumes a P50-Q1 length of 16.55 mm. Measure your pins and change JIG_DEPTH if they differ.
  • Web The 0.57 mm web at 1.27 pitch is the weak point. Don't push pins in at an angle.
  • Fit Flange-in-slot and spine-in-pocket checked by mesh boolean against the original case STL: 0 overlap.
  • Grid Hole positions copied from the original Excellon drill file, not re-drawn.
